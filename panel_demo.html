Why AI phishing is hard to stop

  • Perfect language — the classic "spot the bad grammar" defence is gone.
  • Polymorphism — every message unique; signatures and blocklists decay instantly.
  • Text-only BEC — no link or attachment for gateways to scan or detonate.
  • Look-alike domains and compromised mailboxes defeat SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
  • Scale — ~31,000 attacks/day overwhelm manual SOC triage.
  • Adversarial iteration — attackers test against public filters until the mail passes.
